About VCIL

Passion Beyond Generations

Here, we are family

VCIL is more than a cricket league. It is a family united by cricket, friendship and respect.

“Every great story deserves a second innings.”

“हर खूबसूरत कहानी एक दूसरी पारी की हकदार होती है।”

For years, our players put cricket aside for careers, families and responsibilities. Life moved forward, but the love for the game never left.

Vision Cricket India League (VCIL) brings that passion back to the field, creating a genuine playing platform for 40+, 50+ and 60+ veteran cricketers to compete, connect and live their cricketing dreams once again.

“The game may pause, but the passion never does.”

“खेल रुक सकता है, जुनून नहीं।”

Our mission

To give veteran cricketers real teams, real matches and a real second innings, in a fair, competitive and welcoming cricket community.

Our vision

To build a platform where age is respected, experience is celebrated and every player gets another chance to feel the thrill of the game.

This is not the end of your cricket story.

This is your second innings.

Your Team. Your Family. Your Game. Your Moment.

Vision Cricket India League. Because some dreams deserve another innings.

What We Stand For

  • Second Innings Spirit

    “Some dreams don’t have an expiry date.”

    It’s never too late to step back onto the field, pick up the bat, and live the game you never stopped loving.

  • Inclusivity

    “The game belongs to everyone who carries the spirit of a player.”

    Whatever your age, experience or skill level, there is always a place for you on our field.

  • Camaraderie

    “Cricket may bring us together, but friendship keeps us together.”

    On the field, we compete. Off the field, we celebrate. We are more than teammates, we are one cricket family.

  • Legacy & Respect

    “Age adds years to life; cricket adds memories to those years.”

    We honour the experience, discipline, stories and passion that every veteran brings to the game. Your journey matters. Your legacy matters.

“The years may have passed. The passion never did.”

“यह दूसरी पारी है — लेकिन जुनून वही है।”

Founder's Message

“Some dreams are not meant to retire… they are meant to be lived again.”

I am Sharatchandra, Founder of Vision Cricket India League and, above all, simply a cricket lover.

I have never claimed to be a great cricketer, but the love for the game never left me. I started playing cricket again, professionally wherever possible, through different clubs and organisations across India.

Somewhere on that journey, an idea was born. Why should age stop a cricketer from living his dream?

I wanted to create a platform where players aged 40+, 50+ and 60+ could come together, play competitive cricket, travel to different cities, make friendships and, most importantly, enjoy the game they have loved all their lives.

That is how Vision Cricket India League, VCIL, was born.

“यह सिर्फ क्रिकेट खेलने का मंच नहीं है,
यह उन सपनों को दोबारा जीने का मंच है, जो कभी अधूरे रह गए।”

My dream is to turn my passion into a cricket family, a family where every veteran gets a fair opportunity. And hopefully, very soon, we will see our veterans representing India in 40+, 50+ and 60+ cricket on national and international platforms.

Sharatchandra SharmaFounder, Vision Cricket India LeagueA Cricket Lover

Questions & Answers

Who can play in VCIL?

VCIL is for veteran cricketers in three categories: 40+, 50+ and 60+. Whatever your experience or skill level, there is a place for you on our field.

I haven't played properly in years. Can I still join?

Yes. VCIL was built for players who put cricket aside for careers, families and responsibilities. The game may pause, but the passion never does.

Is it casual or competitive?

VCIL gives veteran cricketers real teams, real matches and a real second innings, in a fair, competitive and welcoming cricket community.

Which tournaments are coming up?
  • 50+ T20 Season 1: 13–15 November 2026, Soni Stadium, Jaipur
  • 50+ Pink Ball Tournament (40 overs): 17–21 December 2026, Ranveer Singh Stadium and Adnok Cricket Stadium, Jaipur
  • 60+ Pink Ball Tournament (40 overs): 9–12 January 2027, Adnok Cricket Stadium, Soni Stadium and Anantam Cricket Stadium, Jaipur
How much does it cost to take part?
  • 50+ T20 Season 1: team entry ₹31,000, player fee ₹14,500 per player
  • 50+ Pink Ball Tournament: team entry ₹25,000, player fee ₹18,500 per player
  • 60+ Pink Ball Tournament: fees to be announced

The fee includes a stay in a 3-star hotel, food, transportation, a match jersey, the best grounds, and matches live on YouTube with two cameras.

How do I register?

Use the registration form. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. The VCIL team will confirm your registration and share payment details.

Can my family watch the matches?

Yes. Matches are streamed live on our YouTube channel, visioncricketindia, with two cameras.

What is the 100 Ball format?

It's the new format started in England. Overs are 5 balls instead of 6, and a bowler may choose to bowl 10 balls in a row.

Is there anything beyond the cricket?

Yes. The organisers host a local city tour of Jaipur on 19 December during the 50+ Pink Ball Tournament, and on 11 January during the 60+ Pink Ball Tournament.

Can my business sponsor VCIL?

Yes. We offer Title, Associate, Tournament, Team and Supporting Partner packages. Tap Sponsorship to send an enquiry and our team will contact you.
