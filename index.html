Founder's message

I am Sharatchandra Sharma, founder of VCIL. Above all, simply a cricket lover.

I have never claimed to be a cricketer, but the love for the game never left me. Somewhere on that journey, an idea was born: why should age stop a cricketer from living his dream?

So the idea came up to create a platform where players aged 40+, 50+ and 60+ could come together, play competitively, and travel to different cities. This is how VCIL was born.

Sharatchandra Sharma

Our mission

To give veteran cricketers a platform to compete, connect and continue their cricketing journey — with the professionalism, respect and passion they deserve.

Our vision

To create a nationwide veteran-cricket community where age is never a barrier, experience is celebrated, and the passion for cricket never retires.

“Because passion for cricket has no age.”

This is not the end of your cricket story.

This is your second innings.

      Questions & Answers

      Who can play in VCIL?

      VCIL is for veteran cricketers in three categories: 40+, 50+ and 60+. Whatever your experience or skill level, there is a place for you on our field.

      I haven't played properly in years. Can I still join?

      Yes. VCIL was built for players who put cricket aside for careers, families and responsibilities.

      Is it casual or competitive?

      Both. The cricket is competitive and fair, and it is played in the spirit of friendship, fitness and sportsmanship.

      Which tournaments are coming up?
      • 50+ T20 Season 1: 13–15 November 2026, Soni Cricket Stadium and Ranveer Singh Cricket Ground, Jaipur (registration closed). See the fixtures.
      • 50+ 40-Overs Pink Ball Tournament: 17–21 December 2026, Ranveer Singh Stadium and Adnok Cricket Stadium, Jaipur
      • 60+ 40-Overs Pink Ball Tournament: 9–12 January 2027, Adnok Cricket Stadium, Soni Stadium and Anantam Cricket Stadium, Jaipur
      How much does it cost to take part?

      Registration is free for the first year. For team entry and player fees, message us on WhatsApp. What each tournament includes is listed under What's included.

      How do I register?

      Open the Player Registration Form in Registration & Fees, and upload your ID card inside the form. The VCIL team will send you an approval message with the fee payment details. You can also use the quick WhatsApp registration on the same page.

      Can my family watch the matches?

      Yes. Matches are streamed live on our YouTube channel, visioncricketindia, with two cameras.

      Is there anything beyond the cricket?

      Yes. There is a one-day local Jaipur city tour on 19 December during the 50+ 40-Overs Pink Ball Tournament (entry fees, tolls and food on the tour are self-paid), and a one-day local city tour on 11 January during the 60+ 40-Overs Pink Ball Tournament.

      Where can I read the rules?

      The Rules section on the Tournament page has the PDFs. The rules are the same for the 50+ and 60+ formats, apart from the age category.

      Can we order jerseys and apparel for our team?

      Yes. Custom designs are available for all teams. See the design catalogues in the VCIL Shop and message us on WhatsApp on +91 86965 55777 with the design number.

      Can my business sponsor VCIL?

      Yes. We offer Title, Associate, Tournament, Team and Supporting Partner packages. See Become a Partner on the Sponsorship page.
